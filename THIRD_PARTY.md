# Third-party components

The browser runtime bundles Three.js from the existing project vendor directory. Its original MIT license is included at `web/vendor/three/LICENSE` after extracting `browser-runtime.zip`, and at `vendor/three/LICENSE` inside that archive.

Central Icons are owned and licensed separately. The production 21 Media site can provide them through its own integration. Their SVG exports are not part of this repository or its release archives.
